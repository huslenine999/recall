The algorithms come from the cubing community; where from is listed below.

Algorithm sources
  • Megaminx PLL – the full PLL sheet by Raul Low Beattie, using James Macdiarmid’s case system.
  • Megaminx OLL – the OLL algorithms from SpeedcubeQuest (speedcube.quest).
  • Square-1 OBL – HCubist’s OBL sheet by Hitesh Kumar Bhuwalka (hcubist.com).
  • Square-1 PBL – Daniel’s Public PBL Doc.
  • Square-1 CSP – the “Squanmate cube shape + parity” sheet.
  • Skewb NS and Sarah’s Advanced, Pyraminx L4E – HCubist’s sheets by Hitesh Kumar Bhuwalka (hcubist.com).
  • 2x2 PBL, CLL, EG-1, EG-2, LEG-1, TCLL+, TCLL-, LS and TEG2+ – the “Best 2x2 Algs” spreadsheet kept by its editors (best2x2algs.com). Chris Olson (cyotheking.com) made many of the first LEG-1 and TCLL algs; TEG2+ was created by Vilius Ribinskas.
